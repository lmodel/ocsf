# Policies

* [AI covenant](ai-covenant.md) - The rules for AI-assisted work here: whoever submits owns the contribution, an element says what OCSF says, a rule states only what the upstream states, a mapping is read at its source first, and a scheduled agent's change goes through a person's review.
* [Security policy](security-policy.md) - How to report a vulnerability in this repository, by GitHub's private reporting, what executes here and what holds each surface. A problem in the CWE List or its XSD belongs with the CWE Program.
* [Licence and attribution](license-and-attribution.md) - Whose work each part is: the code and the schema under Apache-2.0, the bundle under CC BY 4.0, OCSF's text under its own Apache-2.0 with its NOTICE kept, and no copy of ocsf/examples, which states no licence. No endorsement by the OCSF project is implied.
